## Loop rules

1. Record the judge recommendation and its product consequence.
2. Assign one owner a bounded change or verification; reuse an existing owner.
3. Inspect the resulting source/test/artifact or observed runtime evidence. A completion message alone is insufficient.
4. Mark the exact boundary verified and keep the remaining boundary open.
5. Request another independent judge pass only when a meaningful new integrated capability is ready. Preserve counterexamples and disagreements, and record why a recommendation is deferred.

This ledger does not award scores, certify emergency readiness, or replace the independent judge.
