## Current priorities

1. **P0 — Prove the new single help-call flow:** Pharos answers; consented caller facts and content concerns reach Beacon. Every case remains in the **same urgency queue**, including reported immediate danger and unknown-location follow-up. A review badge is independent of urgency, never a reason to hide or automatically down-rank a distressed caller.
2. **P0 — Enforce current human-review decisions:** `integrityReview.status` is `pending`, `clear`, `review-required`, `accepted`, or `dismissed`. Claim and ETA APIs must block pending/review-required/dismissed cases. Reasoned, current-version human acceptance releases that gate; follow-up ownership remains available for verification. New concern/unavailable analysis can reopen review and invalidate obsolete ETA; same-sequence late results cannot undo a human decision and benign results cannot silently release a hold.
3. **P0 — Keep inference honest:** concrete risky-action-plus-pressure flags are distinct from a narrow explicit fabrication/resource-diversion verification concern. Neither proves a false emergency. Panic, insistence, changed facts, lack of a name/ID, tone, accent, or refusal of recording are not deception evidence. Analysis failure routes to human review with danger still highlighted.
4. **P0 — Add optional private audio safely:** the user now requests a consented recording beside a flagged transcript for human listening. Explicit recording consent, an equal transcript-only option, authenticated playback, 24-hour retention, and withdrawal scrubbing are new **unverified requirements**. No automated tone/pacing fraud score is authorized or evidenced.
5. **P0 — Verify and publish this contract:** old passing suites and the earlier published app do not prove the new review gate or audio path. Verify state/race/privacy/area regressions, coordinated calls, and the served final source stamp. The observed generic-landmark area failure remains open until its fix and full-flow rerun pass.

The confirmed review endpoint is `POST /api/cases/:id/integrity-review` with `decision=accept|dismiss`, a reason of 8–1000 characters, and `expectedVersion`, `expectedSourceCallId`, and `expectedSequence`. The durable review record includes its status, version, source call/sequence, analysis status, basis, reason, signals, timestamp, and decision audit. These are an agreed implementation contract, **not a claim that the current code or tests pass**.

Mandatory edge checks: unavailable/timeout remains visible human review; API bypass/stale decision/replay rejects; late concern after an earlier claim/ETA reopens appropriately without losing accountable ownership; same-sequence delayed results respect human disposition; quoted/negated/hypothetical fabrication and victim reports do not become admissions. Preserve the reported danger and queue position during review. A recording decline must neither block text intake nor create a suspiciousness flag.

Judge assessment: this is a tighter State Farm/Photon coordination story and a feasible beyond-classification workflow. T-Mobile fit must be described as **responder protection from suspicious inbound requests**, not the previously planned resident scam-call protection. Existing consumer-fraud research and six-case model evaluation do not validate lie detection, false-emergency classification, new verification bases, or human judgments from tone.

## Loop rules

1. Record the judge recommendation and its product consequence.
2. Assign one owner a bounded change or verification; reuse an existing owner.
3. Inspect the resulting source/test/artifact or observed runtime evidence. A completion message alone is insufficient.
4. Mark the exact boundary verified and keep the remaining boundary open.
5. Request another independent judge pass only when a meaningful new integrated capability is ready. Preserve counterexamples and disagreements, and record why a recommendation is deferred.

This ledger does not award scores, certify emergency readiness, or replace the independent judge.
