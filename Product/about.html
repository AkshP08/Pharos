About Pharos

Pharos is a HackUTD 2026 prototype for safer disaster support. Flare is the voice-first resident intake path. Beacon is the responder-facing experience for a human-reviewed help queue and a separate suspicious-contact review.

What the demo shows

  • A queue that makes reported urgency clear and keeps missing details visible for human follow-up.
  • A synthetic transcript workbench connected to a persistent backend that creates a tracked call, location-aware case, urgency suggestion, responder eligibility, and separate wording review.
  • A Photon SIP → LiveKit voice-agent implementation for speech-to-text and consented transcript intake. The Photon phone line and SIP route still need setup before real calls can reach it.
  • Pressure Trace, which highlights message wording such as a false deadline, a demand for money or codes, or a request not to verify independently.
  • A corner guide that answers fixed questions about the prototype; it is not connected to a language model or live emergency services.
Prototype limits: The deployed demo contains synthetic examples only. Its transcript workbench writes to a persistent backend; the help form remains a local preview. The Photon/LiveKit phone route is not configured, the system does not intercept calls on a person's usual phone, and no alert, responder dispatch, identity check, or proven scam finding is provided. A human must review every case and warning.

The message checker looks only at wording. It does not analyze a caller’s voice, emotion, or identity. A warning means “verify this independently,” not “this is definitely a scam.”
