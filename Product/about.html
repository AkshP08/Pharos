About SignalBridge

SignalBridge is a HackUTD 2026 prototype for disaster-response support. It brings together a responder view of help requests and a way to review suspicious disaster-related messages.

What the demo shows

  • A queue that makes reported urgency clear and keeps missing details visible for human follow-up.
  • Pressure Trace, which highlights message wording such as a false deadline, a demand for money or codes, or a request not to verify independently.
Prototype limits: All examples are synthetic. This page is not connected to Photon messaging or calling, does not receive real emergency requests, does not identify a sender or prove a scam, and does not dispatch responders. A human must review every case and warning.

The message checker looks only at wording. It does not analyze a caller’s voice, emotion, or identity. A warning means “verify this independently,” not “this is definitely a scam.”
