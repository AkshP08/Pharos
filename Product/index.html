Sample phone conversationFictional example · call audio is not recorded

Pharos: “Speech recognition will process what you say so I can understand your choice. If you agree, Pharos will save a text transcript and brief request summary for human responders. Transcript text is deleted after 24 hours. Say yes to continue. You can hang up any time.”

Caller: “Yes.”

Pharos: “First, are you in immediate danger? You can say yes, no, or not sure.”

Caller: “Yes. Water is coming into the house.”

Pharos: “What nearby address or landmark can responders use?”

Caller: “Near the Riverbend footbridge.”

Pharos: “What help do you need most right now? Is there a time-sensitive need I should include?”

Caller: “A safe way out. There are four of us, and one person needs powered oxygen.”

Pharos: “I heard rising water, the Riverbend footbridge, four people, and a powered medical need. Is that right?”

Caller: “Yes, that’s right.”

Pharos: “Okay. I’ll send this as a request for a human responder to review.”

How it should triage: immediate danger and reported time-sensitive needs are visible with the words that support the order. If the caller can’t answer, the field stays unknown for follow-up; it does not lower their place or end the call.
