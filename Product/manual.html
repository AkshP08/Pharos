User manual

From a caller’s report to a human response decision. A practical guide for the Pharos hotline and Beacon response desk.

Release documentation · November 8, 2026 · HackUTD prototype ·

1. Start here

What Pharos does: collects consented phone reports, organizes reported needs by urgency and service area, and gives responders evidence for review. The current deployment is a demonstration prototype. Claiming a case does not dispatch help.
CallerUses the configured hotline. No resident account, web form, or name is required.
ResponderUses Beacon to review facts, verify concerns, claim eligible cases, and report progress.
CoordinatorCan approve responder profiles in addition to response-desk work.

Open Beacon and sign in with the password supplied privately by the project maintainer. Shared role passwords are prototype access controls; they do not verify individual responder identity. Never put credentials in a transcript, screenshot, or presentation.

Use the toolbar to switch between Response (queue and selected case; hotline overview), Calls (sessions), Evidence (call evidence and pressure trace), Coverage (profiles and registration), and Training (synthetic intake). Tabs organize each module. Inside a selected case, use Overview, Transcript & audio, Review, Response, and History. Reported priority and intake review remain visible above the case tabs. Use Text size A−/A+ or Reset to change reading size, and Dark theme / Light theme to switch appearance. These preferences are saved on this device. Transcripts scroll without dropping earlier lines. Long record lists have FIRST, PREVIOUS, NEXT, and LAST controls; their counters cover records currently loaded in the desk. Filters and changed list contents return to the first page. Paging never changes the selected case. On phones, use Requests and Selected case shortcuts; choose Resources for documentation or sign-out.

2. Caller intake and consent

  1. Call the configured Pharos hotline. This presentation build admits consenting test callers on a private allowlist. Use fictional facts and do not request actual dispatch.
  2. At the opening, choose yes for transcript and caller-audio sharing, transcript only to decline recording, or no to decline sharing.
  3. For a recorded demonstration, say “yes” alone, wait for the next prompt, then describe the situation. Recording starts only after acknowledgment; facts spoken in the same utterance as consent may appear in text without recorded audio.
  4. Describe the current danger, a specific location or landmark, number of people, and help needed. A name is optional. The helper asks for missing facts.
  5. To stop sharing, clearly withdraw consent. Local collection stops and Pharos removes relevant retained evidence; external provider processing has separate limits.

For a callback, consent again and explicitly say it is the same situation. Independently provide the location and what changed. Pharos links only a single eligible recent open report with sufficient matching location; a shared phone or uncertain match can create a separate case.

3. Read the response queue

Use All, Immediate safety, Needs follow-up, and Resolved to inspect requests. The service-area selector filters the shared desk; it is not a permission boundary. Updates normally refresh every 12 seconds; use Refresh queue when checking a recent change.

Suggested tierHow to interpret it
Immediate life-safetyReported trapped/no exit, dangerous indoor flooding, or another explicit life-safety concern.
Time-sensitive needReported medical, medication, or essential powered-equipment need without stronger immediate danger.
Urgent assistanceShelter, transport, evacuation, or supplies without stated immediate danger.
Needs follow-upMissing or uncertain facts requiring clarification.

Select a case to inspect the caller’s words, location, people/needs, priority explanation, current owner, response status, and Case update history. Urgency is suggested from reported facts. It is not validated clinical triage or a measured flood forecast.

Review state and urgency are independent. A report with a concern remains visible at its reported urgency. Fear, anger, accent, missing names, and ordinary urgency are not proof of fraud.

4. Review evidence and make a decision

  1. Read the Review before response panel, exact quoted evidence, source call, and current assessment state.
  2. If consented retained audio is available, choose Load private caller audio and start playback manually. Listen beside the transcript. Tone and pacing provide context, not a deception score. Unload recording clears the player.
  3. Verify facts through appropriate human follow-up. A verification/follow-up owner may clarify a held report.
  4. Enter a meaningful reason (at least eight characters), then choose Accept for response or Dismiss from response.
  5. If the evidence changed while you were reviewing, refresh and review the newest version before submitting again.

Pending, flagged, uncertain, unavailable, or dismissed assessments hold normal assignment and ETA until current-source human acceptance. Acceptance authorizes response work; it does not certify truth. Dismissal retains the case, urgency, and audit history and does not cancel an existing assignment. “No concern identified” means only that reviewed words did not support a concern.

Transcript-only, incomplete upload, expired evidence, and clear completed calls may have no audio. Missing audio is not a fraud signal.

5. Assign and report progress

  1. Select an approved, available responder with matching service area, capability, and capacity.
  2. Choose Claim for responder. Sample responders cannot take live phone reports. A blocked action means eligibility or review requirements need attention.
  3. Use the desk-reported response form to explicitly choose On the way, Delayed, Arrived, or Unable to respond. On the way and Delayed require a fresh estimate of 1–180 minutes.
  4. Save the status. During an active call, Pharos can speak the current server-authored desk update during a quiet gap.
  5. Use Mark for follow-up, Release for reassignment, or Resolve case when appropriate. Review the resulting status and owner.

A claim alone promises no travel or arrival. Time passing never marks a responder arrived. Changed danger, location, coverage, or review evidence can invalidate an earlier ETA; review the new facts and explicitly reconfirm progress.

For a justified priority correction, open the Review case tab and expand Review queue priority, select a tier, and record a reason. New life-safety facts can increase urgency above a previous downgrade.

6. Responder coverage and practice

In Coverage → Register responder, register a fictional responder with organization, service areas, capabilities, availability, and capacity. A coordinator must approve new profiles before they receive cases. Approval is a prototype workflow, not professional credential verification.

Use Training → Synthetic intake and the Process transcript button to rehearse with fictional caller/agent dialogue. Sample transcript processing exercises backend logic; it does not demonstrate a phone call or live speech recognition. The message inspection workbench is a wording-rule demonstration, separate from the contextual live model.

7. Troubleshooting

SymptomAction
Sign-in failsCheck the role password privately with the maintainer; do not share it publicly.
New call not visibleRefresh, select All service areas and All requests, then ask the maintainer to check consent, caller admission, and voice-worker health.
Claim or ETA blockedInspect current review state, responder approval/availability/capability/capacity, and case version. Refresh after concurrent changes.
Callback creates another caseDo not force a merge. Check fresh consent, explicit same-situation intent, substantial location match, and ambiguity or active/closed prior reports.
Audio unavailableCheck explicit audio consent, call completion/upload, retention, and sign-in. A clear completed call discards its audio.
Review unavailableKeep human review active; record a reasoned decision after independent verification. Do not treat failure as clearance.
Estimate disappearedReview new facts, location, coverage, and concern state; explicitly save a fresh valid status.
Hotline does not connectAsk the maintainer to check the allowlist, Photon route, hosted LiveKit worker, and current build. Do not create duplicate workers.

8. Privacy and product limits

Caller audio is caller-only, begins after explicit consent, and is bounded to 180 seconds. Retained review audio is encrypted, privately served to signed-in responders, removed on withdrawal, and expires within 24 hours of its source call. Clear completed calls discard audio. Signing out or switching cases clears the current player.

Locations are spoken reports matched to configured areas, not GPS. Caller ID is unverified. Pharos does not establish whether an emergency is real, sense floods, automatically dispatch emergency services, or verify responder credentials. Resident scam-warning enrollment, push/SMS, and two-party call relay are future work outside this demo.
