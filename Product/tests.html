1. Evidence and release gates

Status is evidence, not a promise. The most recent recorded QA lanes report 200 backend/domain checks, 43 UI contracts, and 108 offline voice tests passing. Those are prior local results tied to their fingerprints. The matrix below specifies acceptance behavior; its rows are not claimed executed simply because related automated tests exist.

Historical evidence includes one real consenting Photon intake and a later successful hosted synthetic WebRTC check. Current-build genuine phone intake, worsening callback, suspicious second call, private recording playback, and authenticated dashboard walkthrough remain manual acceptance gates until observed. Simulation and pasted transcripts cannot close real-phone gates.

The small six-case responder-model evaluation matched all six warning decisions with one tactic-category mismatch. It does not establish field accuracy, truth verification, or a deception-detection rate.

2. Test method and environments

P0: release-critical privacy, safety-state, data-integrity, and core-flow checks. P1: usability and secondary workflow checks. Use dummy credentials and isolated SQLite/Miniflare state for automated checks; consenting teammates and fictional facts for hosted phone acceptance. Never reset shared live intake or expose actual caller evidence in screenshots.

For each execution record: case ID, UTC timestamp, source commit and dirty flag, environment, synthetic/real transport, preconditions, actual result, pass/fail/blocked, evidence location, defect ID, tester, and retest outcome. A blocked provider-dependent step stays blocked. Preserve failures and mismatches.

4. Presentation phone acceptance

  1. Genuine frightened caller: separately consent, then report three people safely waiting near Northbank library Zone 2 needing shelter. Verify facts and benign emotion. After clearance or reasoned acceptance, claim an eligible fictional responder and explicitly save On the way, 10 minutes. Verify spoken desk update during the still-active call.
  2. Worsening callback: after ending the first call, consent again and explicitly report the same situation/location with rising indoor water and no exit. Verify one continuing case, Immediate urgency, retained owner/history, and invalidated/reassessed prior ETA.
  3. Concerning second caller: use a separate consenting caller with fictional concrete unsafe request plus supported pressure, or explicit current fabrication plus diversion. Verify retained urgency, exact evidence, blocked claim/ETA, private consented caller-only playback, and reasoned current-source human decision.

Failure criteria include lost consent/location, duplicate or incorrect merge, invented danger/ETA, emotion-only concern, bypassed server hold, wrong-source/public/pre-consent audio, stale overwrite, or lingering phone legs. Stop and preserve evidence; do not describe a workaround as a passing original flow.
